Ctrl-World case gallery

drag to pan when zoomed

Each panel is a wipe: the predicted rollout on the left of the handle, the recorded clip on the right, at the same instant. Drag the handle by moving the pointer across the panel; drag with the pointer held down to pan when zoomed. Under each frame runs the curve of the metric's per-frame value, teal for the prediction and grey for the recording where the metric has one, with a cursor at the current frame. All videos play in lockstep.
